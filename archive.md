<a id="resident-camp"></a>
### Resident Camp at Camp Snyder

- **Date:** Thursday, June 18 through Sunday, June 21, 2026
- **Location:** Camp William B. Snyder, 6100 Antioch Rd, Haymarket, VA 20169
- **Description:** A three-night resident camp. Scouts spent the days in activities across camp, earned adventure loops, and built independence in a supervised environment.
- **How it ran:** Families caravanned from Fairview Elementary Thursday at 5:30 PM and checked in as a group at the covered pavilion in the main parking lot. The BSA swim test happened immediately after check-in. Scouts slept in 4-person canvas tents on wooden platforms, two per tent, with adults in separate tents. Meals were family style in the dining hall, with families providing only Thursday's bag dinner.
- **Worth knowing next time:** Ticks were bad. Electricity was only available in the dining hall, so adults needed battery backups. Everyone needed a completed [Annual Health and Medical Record, Parts A & B](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/HealthSafety/pdf/680-001_AB.pdf) dated within the past year, and dietary needs had to be submitted through camp registration in advance rather than on site.
- **Reference:** [2026 Parent & Leader Guide (PDF)](https://scoutingevent.com/Download/082179014/OR/2026_Parents__Leaders_Guide.pdf)

<a id="spring-campout"></a>
### Spring Pack Campout

- **Date:** Friday evening, May 29 through Sunday morning, May 31, 2026
- **Location:** Camp Wilson at Pohick Bay Regional Park, Lorton, VA
    - [Driving directions](https://maps.app.goo.gl/1J74m1xVzSWTh4Ms6)
    - [Campsite map](https://www.novaparks.com/sites/default/files/pdf/Camp%20Wilson%20Map.pdf)
        - We were at sites A1, A2, and A3
- **Parking:** On-site at the campsite. No park entry fee for campout attendees.
- **Description:** Our spring family campout - a weekend of outdoor fun, campfires, and quality time with your scouts. All families welcome, no camping experience needed.
- **Schedule:** The core program ran Saturday midday through the Saturday evening campfire. Friday night camping was optional for families who wanted to arrive early and settle in. Sunday morning was pack-up and departure.
    - **Saturday schedule:**
        - 1:00 PM: Opening ceremony
        - 1:15 - 2:00 PM: Pack activities
        - 2:00 - 4:00 PM: Den activities and snack
        - 4:00 - 4:30 PM: Free time / dinner prep
        - 4:30 - 5:30 PM: Pack dinner, cleanup, and s'mores
        - 5:30 - 7:30 PM: Campfire (Class A uniforms) - rank awards, den skits and songs, advancement by rank (parent participation)
        - 7:30 - 9:00 PM: Free time
        - 9:00 PM: Lights out
    - **Sunday schedule:**
        - 6:30 AM: Reveille
        - 7:30 AM: Breakfast
        - 9:00 AM: Final walk-through and cleanup
- **Meals:** The pack provided Saturday dinner and Sunday breakfast. All other meals (Friday dinner, Saturday breakfast and lunch) were on families.
