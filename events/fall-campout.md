# Fall Campout & Bobcat Ceremony

**Friday, October 16 through Sunday, October 18, 2026**
Turkey Run Ridge Campground, Prince William Forest Park, 5475 Turkey Run Rd, Triangle, VA 22172 ([map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/UViLm4zJuguiiTay5))

Our big family campout of the fall, and the weekend our newest scouts earn their Bobcat rank at the Saturday campfire.

If you've never camped before, come anyway. Plenty of our families were new to this a year ago, and we'll help you sort out gear and pick a tent spot.

---

## The basics

- **Who:** All pack families, siblings included. Every family needs at least one parent or guardian along.
- 📝 **RSVP:** [RSVP here](https://forms.gle/xN1H1GfQgFSur1Ns9) by Sunday, October 11, so dens can plan Saturday dinner. Please include any food allergies or dietary needs.
- 🆕 **Planning meeting:** Thursday, October 8, 8:00 to 9:00 PM, online. We'll talk through the schedule and activities for the weekend. All parents are welcome, den leaders especially. The Zoom link is on the "Campout Planning Virtual Meeting" event in Scoutbook and in the pack email.
- **Cost:** The park charges a $20 entrance fee per vehicle. If you have a 4th grader, your family can get a free national parks pass for this school year through the Every Kid Outdoors program.
- **Getting there:** Families drive on their own.

---

## When to come

Come for as much of the weekend as you can. If Saturday afternoon and evening is what works for your family, that's completely fine too.

- **Friday, from 5:00 PM:** Arrive and set up. Friday night is on-your-own family camping, with no pack program, so bring your own dinner.
- **Saturday morning:** Arrive and set up your tent if you're coming Saturday.
- **Saturday, 12:00 PM:** Pack activities start.
- **Sunday morning:** Breakfast, an optional interfaith service, then we pack up and head home.

---

## Schedule

**Saturday**

- 12:00 PM: Opening ceremony
- 12:15 to 2:00 PM: Pack activities, with scouts rotating through stations
- 2:00 PM: Snack
- 2:15 PM: Den activities
- 4:30 PM: Dens start dinner
- 5:00 to 6:00 PM: Dinner
- 6:30 PM: Campfire, including the Bobcat ceremony (field uniforms)
- 9:00 PM: Lights out

**Sunday**

- 6:30 AM: Wake up
- 7:30 AM: Breakfast, cooked by our Arrow of Light scouts
- 8:30 AM: Interfaith service (optional)
- 9:00 AM: Final walk-through and cleanup

Bobcat is the first rank every new Cub Scout earns, whatever their grade. Earning it at a campfire in the woods beats earning it in a cafeteria.

---

## Meals

- **Bring your own:** Friday dinner (if you're coming Friday), Saturday breakfast, and Saturday lunch.
- **Saturday dinner:** Each den plans and cooks dinner for its own families. Your den leader will be in touch. Bring a camp stove or cooler if your den's plan needs one.
- **Sunday breakfast:** Cooked by our Arrow of Light scouts for the whole pack.
- **S'mores:** The pack puts them out for everyone Saturday night. Labeled roasting sticks are handy.

---

## What to bring

**For your scout**

- Field uniform for the ceremonies and the campfire, plus a pack activity shirt for Saturday
- Cub Scout six essentials: first-aid kit, water bottle, whistle, flashlight, snack, and sunscreen or bug spray
- Mess kit (plate, cup, bowl, utensils)
- Hiking boots or sturdy sneakers. No open-toed shoes.
- Pocket knife only for Bears, Webelos, and Arrow of Light scouts who have completed the knife safety elective for their current rank, and only with your permission

**For your family**

- Tent, ground tarp, and a mallet for stakes
- Sleeping bags, pads or cots, and pillows. October nights here drop into the 40s, so bring warmer bedding than you think you need.
- Layers, including a warm jacket, a hat, gloves, and rain gear
- Flashlight or headlamp for every person, and a lantern
- Camp chairs and filled water bottles
- Toiletries, towels, and any medications

Don't have a tent? Contact Cubmaster Kevin before the campout. We can usually pair you up with another family or find you something to borrow.

---

## Ground rules

- No alcohol. It isn't allowed at scout events.
- We'd like scouts off phones, tablets, and other screens for the weekend. If your scout needs screen time to settle down, keep it in your tent and just for your scout.

---

## About the park

Prince William Forest Park is a National Park Service site in Triangle, Virginia, with hiking trails and campgrounds throughout.

- Cell service can be unreliable, so sort out any coordination with other families before you arrive.
- Everything you pack in comes back out with you.

Questions? Contact Cubmaster Kevin.
