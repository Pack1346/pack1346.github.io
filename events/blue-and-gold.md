# Blue and Gold Banquet & Arrow of Light Crossover

**Date:** Saturday, March 7, 2026

**Time:** All day event

**Location:** Redeeming Grace Church, 5200 Ox Rd, Fairfax, VA 22030

---

## What to Know

The Blue and Gold Banquet is one of the biggest events of the Cub Scout year. We celebrate our scouts' achievements and recognize advancement. This year's banquet also includes the Arrow of Light Crossover ceremony for our graduating scouts moving on to Scouts BSA.

All families are encouraged to attend. More details on schedule, food, and what to bring will be shared as the date approaches.
