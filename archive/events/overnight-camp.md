# Cub Scout Overnight Camp

**Date:** June 18-21, 2026

**Location:** Camp Snyder, Haymarket, VA

---

## What to Know

Camp Snyder is our council's Cub Scout camp, located in Haymarket, VA. This is a multi-night camping experience designed for Cub Scouts of all rank levels. Scouts participate in a range of activities including archery, nature hikes, swimming, and campfire programs.

Registration details and packing lists will be shared when available. Start thinking about scheduling now - this is a midweek camp (Wednesday through Saturday).
