# Webelos & Arrow of Light Overnight Camp

**Date:** July 12-18, 2026

**Location:** Goshen Scout Reservation, Goshen, VA

---

## What to Know

This week-long overnight camp is for rising 4th and 5th graders (Webelos and Arrow of Light scouts). Goshen Scout Reservation is a premier camping facility in the Blue Ridge Mountains offering a wide range of outdoor programs.

This is a full week commitment. Registration details, costs, and packing information will be shared as they become available. Plan ahead - families should start thinking about summer schedules now.
