# Welcome to Pack 1346

We're a Cub Scout pack based in the Fairview Elementary community and serving families throughout the Fairfax area. Our pack is chartered by Living Savior Lutheran Church and is part of the National Capital Area Council (NCAC).

All families are welcome, whether or not your child attends Fairview. If you're curious about scouting or ready to join, we'd love to hear from you.

**What you'll find here:** Pack-wide events such as campouts, service projects, pack meetings, and summer camp. This page is primarily focused on events that involve the whole pack.

**What's not here:** Den meetings - those are coordinated by individual den leaders. For the full pack calendar, including den meetings and all pack activities, see [Scoutbook Plus](https://advancements.scouting.org/).

### Resident Camp at Camp Snyder

- **Date:** Thursday, June 18, 6:00 PM through Sunday, June 21, 9:00 AM
- **Location:** Camp William B. Snyder, 6100 Antioch Rd, Haymarket, VA 20169 ([directions](https://maps.app.goo.gl/snyder))
- **Description:** A multi-day resident camp for scouts. Scouts spend the days in activities all over camp, earn adventure loops, and build independence in a safe, supervised environment.
- **Medical forms (action needed):** Every camper, scouts and adults, needs a completed [Annual Health and Medical Record, Parts A & B (PDF)](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/HealthSafety/pdf/680-001_AB.pdf). These are parent-completed - no doctor visit. Forms must be dated within the past year and list any prescription medications. Bring them to Thursday check-in; they're returned at check-out.
- **Dietary needs (action needed):** Requests for medical or religious diets must be submitted in advance through camp registration - the kitchen can't accommodate on-site requests. For severe allergies, bring supplemental food in sealed, labeled containers for the camp fridge. If you have an unregistered dietary need, contact the cubmaster ASAP.
- **Arrival (Thursday):** Meet at Fairview Elementary at 5:30 PM to caravan - we check in as a group at the covered pavilion in the main parking lot. Pack a bag dinner; scouts can eat on the way or after the swim test. A limited number of vehicles can unload gear at the campsite, then return to the main lot. Can't make Thursday? Late check-in is available Friday morning.
- **Swim test:** Right after check-in, everyone heads to the pool for the BSA swim test. Wear your swimsuit for the drive or pack it and a towel where you can grab them easily.
    - Attempting the test is required for swimming adventures. Passing is required for boating adventures and for deep-end and slide access - adults included. Anyone who doesn't pass can still play in the shallow area.
    - The test: jump feet first into water over your head, swim 75 yards using any strong stroke (including one turn), swim 25 yards using elementary backstroke, then float motionless.
    - Goggles likely won't be allowed during the test (fine for regular pool time), so prepare your scout if they usually swim with them.
- **Meals:** Served family style in the dining hall. Thursday's bag dinner is the only meal families provide.
- **Sleeping:** 4-person canvas tents on wooden platforms - 2 for scouts, 2 for adults (men/women). Each camper gets a cot, with personal items stored underneath. A bug net for the cot is highly recommended.
- **What to bring:**
    - Bag dinner for Thursday, day pack, water bottle, hat, sunscreen, bug spray (ticks are bad at Snyder!)
    - Swimsuit, towels (pool and shower), water shoes and flip flops (pool, waterfront, and showers only - hard-soled, closed-toed shoes everywhere else in camp)
    - Scout uniform shirt (worn for evening flags), t-shirts, shorts, one pair of pants, underwear, socks, sweatshirt or light jacket, raincoat or poncho, sneakers or hiking boots
    - Sleeping bag and flat sheet, pillow, bug net, lightweight bedding
    - Toiletries, basic first aid, medications, flashlight, trading post money
    - Battery backup charger for adult phones (electricity is only in the dining hall). No electronics for scouts.
- **Do not bring:** Phones or electronics for scouts, bicycles, pets, BB guns or archery equipment, sheath knives, fireworks, or anything you'd hate to lose - camp isn't responsible for lost or damaged property.
- **Carry what you need for the day:** We'll rarely return to the campsite during the day. Wear sturdy walking shoes and carry a day pack with water, snacks, swimsuit and towel, a plastic bag for wet clothes, basic first aid, any daytime medications, and a small bag chair if desired.
- **Beat the heat:** Days will be hot and sweaty, with temps staying high into the evening. Small towels to wet for your neck, spray bottles, or battery-powered fans are worth packing.
- **Showers:** Separate facilities for boys, girls, men, and women - scouts shower and change on their own. A shower towel and flip flops make a cooldown shower easy.
- **Parent & Leader Guide:** [2026 Parent & Leader Guide (PDF)](https://scoutingevent.com/Download/082179014/OR/2026_Parents__Leaders_Guide.pdf) - full rules and regulations.
- **Sample daily schedule:** [Last year's sample schedule (PDF)](https://scoutingevent.com/Download/082159735/OR/Residenty_Weekend_Sample_Schedule_2025.pdf) - from 2025, to give you a sense of the activities and pace. We'll post this year's if one becomes available.
- **Questions?** Contact Cubmaster Kevin at lybarger@gmail.com or 206-491-6309.
