# Welcome to Pack 1346

We're a Cub Scout pack based in the Fairview Elementary community and serving families throughout the Fairfax area. Our pack is chartered by Living Savior Lutheran Church and is part of the National Capital Area Council (NCAC).

All families are welcome, whether or not your child attends Fairview. If you're curious about scouting or ready to join, we'd love to hear from you.

---

## Join Pack 1346

Cub Scouts is open to kids in kindergarten through 5th grade, and you can join any time of year. You don't need any camping or outdoor experience, and we don't expect it from parents either.

- **Ready to sign up?** [Register online here](https://my.scouting.org/VES/OnlineReg/1.0.0/?tu=UF-MB-082paa1346)
- **Want more information, or have a question?** [Fill out our interest form](https://tinyurl.com/4edf2vmp) and we'll get back to you.
- **Want to just come see?** Visit our [November Pack Meeting](#november-pack-meeting) on Tuesday, November 17. Bring a friend, no commitment.
- **Follow along:** [Pack 1346 on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/Pack1346)

## Invitations From Local Troops

Scouts BSA troops near us sometimes invite our families to their events. These are the troop's events, not the pack's. They're optional, there's no pack signup, and families go on their own. They're also a fun, low-pressure way to see what a troop is like, especially for Webelos and Arrow of Light families.
